1. **What is Capri DMC's relationship with Nesea Capri Tours & Events?**  
   *Capri DMC is the dedicated Business-to-Business (B2B) luxury spin-off from Nesea Capri Tours & Events, created specifically to handle high-touch VIP logistics, private fleet charters, and confidential net-rate arrangements for travel designers.*

2. **Does Capri DMC work directly with travel advisors and Virtuoso agencies?**  
   *Yes. Capri DMC operates on protected wholesale net rates with 100% white-label capability upon request, serving travel advisors across the US, UK, Europe, Middle East, and Asia with 24/7 on-island emergency response.*

3. **What boat charter services does Capri DMC guarantee during peak season?**  
   *Capri DMC maintains priority access to traditional wooden Gozzo Caprese, high-speed Riva motor yachts, and private catamarans, guaranteeing availability even during peak July and August demand.*

4. **Can Capri DMC orchestrate Jewish milestone celebrations and Kosher logistics?**  
   *Yes. Capri DMC specializes in milestone lifecycle events including Bar Mitzvah, Bat Mitzvah, and Kiddushin ceremonies on Capri, coordinating scenic cliffside Torah readings and strictly supervised Kosher catering.*
